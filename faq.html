Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our tutors, programmes, pricing, and learning process.

About Our Tutors
1

Are your tutors background-checked?

Yes, every tutor completes identity verification and a background check before being matched with a family.
Services
2

What curricula and tuitions do you offer?

We offer support for CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE, IB, and Tuitions for KG—Plus Two.
3

Can classes be conducted in Malayalam or Hindi alongside English?

Yes, our mother-teachers can explain concepts in Malayalam or Hindi alongside English instruction, especially for younger students.
Scheduling & Logistics
4

What happens if we relocate to a different state?

Tuition continues uninterrupted — our online format means your child keeps the same tutor and schedule regardless of which state you move to.
5

How do you track and report a child's academic progress?

Parents receive progress updates after each session and a detailed monthly report covering topics covered, strengths, and areas for improvement.
Pricing & Payments
6

What is the fee structure?

We offer both monthly and term-based plans. Full pricing is available on our Pricing page.

Still have questions?

Our team is ready to help you find the perfect tutor for your child.
